Privacy Policy

Your privacy is our top priority

Last updated: December 12, 2024

🔒 Privacy First Approach

All processing happens locally in your browser. Your documents never leave your device. We don't collect, store, or transmit your content.

1. Introduction

Welcome to MarkdownPDF ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your data. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our Markdown to PDF conversion tool.

2. Information We DON'T Collect

Unlike most web services, MarkdownPDF is designed with privacy at its core:

  • ❌ We do NOT collect your Markdown content
  • ❌ We do NOT store your PDF files
  • ❌ We do NOT track your document content
  • ❌ We do NOT upload your files to any server
  • ❌ We do NOT require registration or login
  • ❌ We do NOT sell your data to third parties

3. How Our Service Works

MarkdownPDF operates entirely in your web browser:

  • ✅ All Markdown parsing happens on your device
  • ✅ PDF generation occurs locally in your browser
  • ✅ Your documents never leave your computer
  • ✅ No server-side processing of your content
  • ✅ Works completely offline once loaded

4. Local Storage

We use your browser's local storage to enhance your experience:

What We Store Locally:

  • Your Markdown content (auto-save feature)
  • Your page size and orientation preferences
  • Your zoom level settings

Important Notes:

  • This data is stored only on YOUR device
  • We cannot access this data
  • You can clear it anytime via browser settings
  • It's automatically deleted if you clear browser data

5. Analytics and Cookies

Basic Analytics:

We may use privacy-friendly analytics to understand:

  • Number of visitors
  • Pages visited
  • General geographic location (country level)
  • Browser type and device type

We do NOT track:

  • Your document content
  • Individual user behavior
  • Personal information

Cookies:

We use minimal cookies only for:

  • Essential website functionality
  • Remembering your preferences

We do NOT use cookies for advertising or tracking.

6. Third-Party Services

Our website may use the following third-party services:

  • Google Fonts: For typography (no tracking)
  • CDN Services: For loading libraries (Marked.js, html2pdf.js)

These services have their own privacy policies. We choose privacy-respecting services whenever possible.

7. Data Security

We take security seriously:

  • ✅ HTTPS encryption for all connections
  • ✅ No server-side data storage
  • ✅ Client-side processing only
  • ✅ No user accounts or passwords
  • ✅ Regular security updates

8. Children's Privacy

Our service does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. Since we don't collect personal information at all, our service can be safely used by people of all ages.

9. GDPR Compliance

For users in the European Union:

  • We don't process personal data, so GDPR requirements are minimal
  • You have full control over your local data
  • No data portability needed (everything is already on your device)
  • Right to erasure: Clear your browser data

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:

  • Updating the "Last updated" date at the top
  • Posting the new policy on this page

Continued use of the service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us:

  • Email: connect@rivonixtech.com
  • Contact Form: Contact Page

Summary

MarkdownPDF is built with privacy by design. Your documents are processed entirely on your device, and we have no access to your content. We believe in transparency and giving you full control over your data.
