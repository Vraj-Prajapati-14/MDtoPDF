## 🎨 4. Text Colors (Modern Rule)

### ❌ Avoided
- Pure black (#000000) - Too harsh

### ✅ Using
- **Body Text**: #333333 (soft dark gray)
- **Headings**: #1F3A8A (dark blue)
- **Strong Text**: #222222 (darker gray)
- **Code**: #1F3A8A (dark blue)

**Result**: Easy on the eyes, professional appearance

---

## 📦 5. Boxes & Highlights

### Blockquotes (Highlight Boxes)
- **Background**: #EEF2FF (very light blue) ✅
- **Border**: 2pt left border, dark blue (#1F3A8A) ✅
- **Padding**: 10pt ✅
- **Border Radius**: 3px (subtle rounded corners) ✅
- **Font Style**: Normal (not italic) ✅

**Usage**: Key takeaways, important notes, summaries

---

## 📏 6. Borders (Minimal Design)

### ❌ NOT Using
- Page border lines
- Decorative frames
- Thick borders

### ✅ Using
- **H1**: 0.5pt thin divider line
- **H2**: 0.5pt subtle divider line
- **Tables**: 0.5pt borders
- **Code Blocks**: 0.5pt border + 2pt left accent

**Result**: Clean, modern, minimal

---

## 🔹 7. Underline Usage

### ❌ Avoided
- Underlining normal text (looks like hyperlink)

### ✅ Using
- **Links only**: Subtle underline with light blue color

**Alternative**: Bold text for emphasis instead of underline
